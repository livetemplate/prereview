# Cut empty preamble

Find the phrases and sentences that add nothing: ones that defend what the text already
says, announce what comes next, or prepare the reader for it instead of saying it. Cut
them, or fold the one useful word into the sentence it introduces. Keep everything that
carries information.

Propose each cut as a `prereview suggest` edit (a before→after on the exact lines) so I
can accept or reject it. Do not modify files directly.
